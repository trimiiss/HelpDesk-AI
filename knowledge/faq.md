# Demo Auto Service FAQ

## Opening hours

We are open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00, and Saturday from 09:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## Location and parking

Demo Auto Service is at Rruga Demo 12 in Prishtina, next to the city ring road. Customers can park for free in front of the workshop.

## Booking an appointment

Appointments are booked by phone during opening hours. Most services can be done within two working days. Oil changes and tyre changes can also be done without an appointment, but you may have to wait.

## Payment

We accept cash, debit cards and credit cards. We do not offer payment by instalments.

## Courtesy car

A courtesy car is available for repairs that take longer than one day. It has to be reserved when you book, and it costs 15 euros per day.

## Cancelling an appointment

Please cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. There is no fee for cancelling on time. If you do not come and did not cancel, we charge 10 euros.

## Which cars we work on

We work on all petrol and diesel cars and on light vans up to 3.5 tonnes. We do not work on motorcycles, trucks or fully electric cars.
